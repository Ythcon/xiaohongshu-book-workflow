# Sources, Copy, and QA

## Minimum Verification

Confirm from reliable sources:

- exact title and subtitle;
- author, editor, or interviewer roles;
- publisher, publication year, and edition used;
- real cover image;
- names and locations of depicted projects or artifacts;
- photographer/creator and rights statement where available.

Prefer primary sources and institutions. A search result, repost, Pinterest board, or image CDN is not a sufficient final citation.

## Image Rights Record

Create `图片来源.md` with one row per downloaded or reproduced asset:

```text
filename | depicted content | author/institution | source URL | license/copyright | modifications
```

If reuse permission is unclear, state that plainly. Do not convert “source found” into “free to reuse.” Keep editorial sourcing notes separate from rights claims.

## Covers

Use a verified edition cover, preferably from the publisher, author/foundation, library catalog, or bookseller page that names the edition. Do not redraw the title, replace cover typography, invent a cover, or silently use a different edition. Scaling and cropping outer blank margins are acceptable; distortion is not.

## Text Integrity

- Use direct quotation marks only for text checked against the book or a primary source.
- Label translations, paraphrases, and editorial summaries.
- Avoid claims about intention unless the source supports them.
- Keep names, dates, and project titles consistent across cards, post copy, and source record.

## Visual QA

Inspect the contact sheet and individual problem cards:

- 1242 x 1660, RGB, JPG;
- readable at phone size;
- safe margins and no clipped glyphs;
- no stretched cover or photograph;
- no low-resolution asset enlarged past usefulness;
- no decorative line crosses faces, buildings, drawings, or essential labels;
- 02–05 share a family without becoming copies;
- 01 and 06 are structurally specific to the current book;
- visible page numbers and consistent naming.

## Publish Copy

Aim for:

- title: about 20 Chinese characters, not clickbait detached from the book;
- body: 300–500 Chinese characters with claim, evidence, and reader value;
- tags: 6–10 precise tags covering designer, book, discipline, and method.

The copy should state when the post is an editorial reading rather than an official summary.
